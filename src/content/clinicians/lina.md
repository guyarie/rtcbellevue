## _My Approach: Somatic and Attachment-Based Healing_

_Relationships are at the heart of how we experience both suffering and healing. Many of the clients who come to see me have spent years trying to understand their anxiety, depression, trauma, or relationship struggles through insight alone, yet continue to feel stuck in the same emotional and physical patterns. My work is grounded in the belief that healing happens not only through understanding our stories, but also through reconnecting with the wisdom of the body and the safety of authentic human connection._

_Drawing from advanced training in Sensorimotor Psychotherapy, developed by Dr. Pat Ogden, I help clients explore how trauma, stress, and attachment experiences live within the nervous system. Trauma often leaves behind more than memories—it can shape the way we breathe, move, respond to conflict, trust others, set boundaries, and experience ourselves in relationships. These patterns are not signs of weakness; they are adaptive survival strategies that once helped us cope._

_In our work together, we gently bring awareness to bodily sensations, emotions, thoughts, impulses, and relational experiences as they arise in the present moment. Rather than reliving overwhelming events, we learn to listen to what the body has been holding and support the nervous system in discovering new experiences of safety, choice, and connection._

_Attachment-focused therapy recognizes that our earliest relationships shape how we see ourselves and others. Whether you are struggling with PTSD, Complex PTSD, relationship difficulties, family conflict, or the lasting effects of childhood adversity, therapy offers an opportunity to develop a more secure relationship with yourself and with the people who matter most. Through a collaborative, compassionate process, we work to transform patterns of protection into experiences of resilience, self-trust, and meaningful connection._

_I bring to this work a deep respect for each person’s unique history, culture, and strengths. Having lived across different cultures and languages myself, I understand that healing is never one-size-fits-all. My role is not to tell you who you should become, but to help you reconnect with the wisdom, resilience, and capacity for healing that already exist within you._

## About Elina

_I love my work. It’s an honor and a privilege to support people in creating positive change in their lives._

_My professional mission has been to partner with people who experience personal crisis, depression, anxiety, addictions, uncertainty, loss, or trauma, using a relational, attachment lens to understand them._

_It has been a personal privilege and a professional joy to be able to help people seeking new life goals, improved clarity and energy with a mindful, trauma sensitive, validating and strength-based approach. After years of working as an on-site therapist at a local school, my goal has also become to promote greater acceptance of our human diversity and to support people who find themselves all over the rainbow of the Human Spectrum._

_The years of professional training help me address many aspects of emotional and thought disorders, as well as issues resulting from the legacy of trauma. My personal experiences as a former Soviet refugee and a child immigrant in Israel inform my understanding of struggling that runs more than skin-deep. I chose a graduate degree in Social Work to deepen my knowledge of the societal processes that augment and aggravate deeply personal problems._

_I am an energetic and caring person, comfortable with people of widely differing life experiences and aspirations, and skilled at meeting people where they are, holding a safe space for them to practice self-compassion and self-care, and advocating for them in time of struggle and confusion._

_In my spare time I parent, hike, walk my dog, study and try new hobbies – there is always something new and interesting on the horizon._
