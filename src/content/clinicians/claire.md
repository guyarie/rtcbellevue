## Types of Services

### Child Therapy and Parent Support

_Children are amazing individuals learning and engaging in their world. At this time, they are still developing the language and interpretation of how the world works. I provide a structured, safe place and engage children with play therapy. I use a variety of materials and activities to help them understand and process their emotions, build social and emotional skills, and resolve psychological and interpersonal conflicts._

### Individual Therapy for Teens and Adults

_I focus on rapport and building your trust to learn about your world and how this has impacted you. I do my best to support you in prioritizing internal and external safety, identifying your needs, learning and practicing how to regulate and repair ruptures within you and your relationships with others._

### Consultation and Collaboration

_I value the importance of having a cohesive understanding of you or your child’s needs. I do my best to make time to observe, coordinate care and seek collateral information in order to improve clinical outcomes and ensuring holistic care._

### Supervision

_Our work together is a structured, individualized relationship-based process where we learn and value our clients’ and our own experiences, and help develop skills to ensure ethical practice and support our personal and professional well-being._

## About Claire

I found and learned the value of support, resources, and strength not just from my own experience, but from clients, families, and colleagues. I am grateful to have work experiences in non-profit agencies, and working with the team and supervisees.
