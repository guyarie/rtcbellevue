### Approach to Therapy for Teens & Families

_As a clinical psychologist and psychoanalyst, I see the sources of mental suffering as complex, multidetermined, and often deeply rooted in one's life history. Through careful listening and reflection, I work to foster an atmosphere of safety wherein people can grow in their capacities for self-understanding, emotional well-being, and self-direction._

_If you're interested in potentially working with me, please reach out. I'd be happy to talk to you more about the kind of help you are seeking and what I might be able to offer._

### About David

Dr. Neal holds a Doctorate in Clinical Psychology (PsyD) from George Fox University, Oregon. He completed his internship at the Institute of Living in Hartford, Connecticut and he is a graduate of the ACPEinc accredited Adult Psychoanalytic Training Program and Post-doctoral Fellowship in Hospital-based Psychotherapy at the Austen Riggs Center, Massachusetts. He is also currently enrolled in the UMass Chan Medical School Fellowship in Early Relational Health (anticipated graduation, 2024).

Having worked in private practice, schools, medical centers, and hospital-based and residential treatment settings, Dr. Neal has experience treating people with a variety of problems including but not limited to anxiety, depression, dissociation, posttraumatic stress, relationship problems, personality dysfunction, and psychosis. He has written and presented on psychoanalysis, metacognition, psychosis, and complex personality problems.

He is interested in human development across the lifespan, environmental and historical contributions to emotional and psychological distress, and the challenges inherent in living and working in groups and social systems.
