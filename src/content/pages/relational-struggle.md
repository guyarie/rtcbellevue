  - theme: bright
    heading: "Our Approach"
    body: |
      **Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)** — Helps identify and shift negative interaction cycles to build a more secure emotional bond.

      **Attachment-Based Therapy** — Explores how early relational patterns shape current ways of connecting and conflict.

      **Communication & Conflict-Resolution Skills** — Practical tools for expressing needs, listening effectively, and de-escalating conflict.

      **Family Systems** — Looks at roles and patterns within the family or relationship system to support healthier dynamics.

      **IFS (Internal Family Systems)** — Explores the different "parts" within each person to build self-compassion and internal harmony, which supports healthier relational patterns.

      **Psychoeducation** — Helps clients understand relational patterns and what to expect from the therapeutic process.
