      ### Confidence in Relationships Group for Adults

      group sessions are in person - Bellevue, WA

      Are the relationships you are part of today not feel satisfying or fulfilling enough? Do you feel lonely, disconnected, or misunderstood? Do you feel people do not get to meet your “real you”?

      If you want to improve your relationships, understand your emotions better, and communicate more effectively, you might benefit from group therapy.

      In this group, you will join other adults who are interested in exploring their thoughts and feelings in a safe and supportive space. The group will offer the opportunity to connect with others more authentically and experience more vulnerability, intimacy, and closeness.

      You will also learn how you impact others and how they impact you; recognize what emotions are triggered and where they come from; get feedback on how others perceive you and what roles you tend to play in relationships. The group will give you the chance to develop awareness and practice new ways of relating and communicating with others inside and outside the group.

      ### The Small Print

      - **When:** Thursdays, 6:00–7:30 p.m. Pacific, weekly in Bellevue.
      - **Who and how many:** Up to eight adults. All genders, races, and identities are welcome.
      - **Fees:** $75 per 90-minute session and $230 for the intake meeting. Contact us for details about reduced fees.
      - **Structure:** This is an ongoing group with an initial six-month commitment and the option to continue.

      ### Registration process

      1. [15-minute initial meeting](https://socialendeavors.sessionshealth.com/) - free
      2. An hour-long Intake meeting – costs: $230, and will conclude with a determination of fit to the group.
      3. Sign participation agreement.
