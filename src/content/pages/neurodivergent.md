      _At Relational Therapy Collective, we understand that neurodivergent clients — including those with ADHD, autism spectrum conditions, and other forms of neurocognitive difference — often benefit from a therapeutic approach that is affirming rather than pathologizing._

      _Treatment is tailored to the individual's cognitive and sensory profile, focusing on identifying personal strengths, developing practical strategies for executive functioning and emotional regulation, and addressing co-occurring concerns such as anxiety, masking, or burnout. Care is collaborative, with treatment goals shaped by the client's own priorities rather than a one-size-fits-all model._

  - theme: bright
    heading: "Our Approach"
    body: |
      - Strengths-based, affirming care that avoids pathologizing neurodivergent traits
      - Individualized treatment plans shaped by each client's goals and profile
      - Support for executive functioning skills (planning, organization, task initiation)
      - Emotional regulation strategies tailored to sensory and cognitive needs
      - Treatment for co-occurring concerns (anxiety, depression, burnout)
      - Communication approaches that respect each client's preferred style
      - Collaborative goal-setting, rather than a one-size-fits-all model
      - Support navigating accommodations at school, work, or home
      - Family/caregiver involvement when appropriate, especially for younger clients
      - **Attachment-Based Therapy** — Addresses anxiety rooted in relational patterns
      - **Mindfulness-Based Approaches** — Builds present-moment awareness to reduce symptoms of trauma
      - **Psychodynamic Approaches** — Explores deeper roots of anxiety and stress
      - **Somatic Techniques** — Manages the physical symptoms of trauma focusing on the nervous system and physical sensations
      - **Psychoeducation** —Provides learning in the nature of trauma and how treatment works.
