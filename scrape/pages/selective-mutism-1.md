## _Selective Mutism_

![A young girl with long blonde hair, wearing a denim jacket and jeans, sitting on the grass near a tree, looking distressed or upset.](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/691bd5695eb667239e7f4e68/8fcaff36-a09f-499a-aa2f-3d5fc82c5c40/selective+mutism.webp)

_Selective mutism (SM) is a childhood anxiety disorder where a person is unable to speak in certain social situations, such as schools or in public, despite being able to speak comfortably in other settings like at home. When anxious, children, teens or adults may appear frozen, stiff or expressionless. they may be avoiding eye contact and rely on nodding, pointing, or whispering._

_It is not a behavioral choice, but a consistent failure to speak that interferes with daily life, often first appearing before age five. Treatment typically involves therapy, like controlled exposure (fade in/fade out technique, bug-in-the-ear coaching), and sometimes medication for severe cases._ 

_Our therapists are RTC are passionate and understand the debilitating impact selective mutism has on an individual. We are dedicated working with children, teens and adults and their families and community to provide education and support on selective mutism._
