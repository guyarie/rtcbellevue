_At Relational Therapy Collective, we understand that trauma affects every aspect of life. Whether you're dealing with a single painful event or long-term complex trauma. Our therapists provide a compassionate space to process difficult experiences and begin healing._

_Trauma therapy helps manage emotional, physical, and relational impacts of your experiences. If you experience intrusive memories, flashbacks, anxiety, numbness, or feel stuck, our trauma-informed therapists use evidence-based approaches to help you reclaim your story, restore connections, and build resilience._

## _Our Approach_

-   PTSD symptoms and traumatic stress reactions
    
-   Flashbacks, nightmares, or intrusive memories
    
-   Anxiety, panic, and hypervigilance
    
-   Emotional numbness, dissociation, or disconnection
    
-   Difficulty trusting others or feeling safe in relationships

**EMDR** (Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing) — structured bilateral stimulations, to help process traumatic memories, reducing their emotional charge.

**DBT** (Dialectical Behavior Therapy) Informed — Distress tolerance, emotion regulation, interpersonal effectiveness, and mindfulness practices

**Mindfulness-Based Approaches** — Builds present-moment awareness to reduce symptoms of trauma

-   Depression or grief related to traumatic events
    
-   Self-blame, shame, or guilt
    
-   Somatic symptoms (body-based trauma responses)
    
-   Childhood trauma and lasting effects on adult life
    
-   Intergenerational and cultural trauma

**Attachment-Based Therapy** — Addresses anxiety rooted in relational patterns

**Psychodynamic Approaches** — Explores deeper roots of anxiety and stress

**Somatic Techniques** — Manages the physical symptoms of trauma focusing on the nervous system and physical sensations

**Psychoeducation** —Provides learning in the nature of trauma and how treatment works.
