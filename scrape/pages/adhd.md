## _ADHD_

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/691bd5695eb667239e7f4e68/1780594743187-NKPOL8KB1KHE4SEV6THC/unsplash-image-tvc5imO5pXk.jpg)

_ADHD, or Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder, is a neurodevelopmental condition affecting a child's ability to focus, manage impulses, and regulate activity levels. Children with ADHD may struggle with attention, organisation, following instructions, or controlling fidgeting and restlessness._

_This can affect family dynamics, create stress between parents, and require different parenting approaches. We help families understand ADHD and develop strategies to support their child while strengthening the parent relationship._

## _What We Help With_

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/691bd5695eb667239e7f4e68/1785908583659-PWWOOKO0KQ4CYAPDN25N/unsplash-image-i14h2xyPr18.jpg)

## _Our Approach_

-   Emotional regulation and frustration tolerance
    
-   Impulse control and flexibility
    
-   Social communication and turn-taking
    
-   Managing overwhelm and emotional intensity
    
-   Executive functioning skills such as planning, organization and follow-through
    
-   Strengthening self-esteem & self-understanding

-   **Understanding Family Dynamics:** and how they impact each others.
    
-   **Behavior Management:** Understanding the function of the behavior and supporting the child to find healthier ways to meet his/her needs.
    
-   **Transitions:** Navigating divorce, moving, new siblings, or school changes.
    
-   **Neurodiversity Support:** Strategies for children with ADHD, autism (ASD) and sensory sensitivities.
    
-   **Communication:** Identifying and learning communication styles and promote positive communication skills.
    
-   **Parental Well-being:** Managing caregiver burnout, stress, and energy.
