### Women in Midlife Process Group

![Silhouettes of five women sitting outdoors during sunset, facing away from the camera, wearing warm clothing.](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/691bd5695eb667239e7f4e68/1770766466787-SVJC7I1AY7NUMYK530IQ/unsplash-image-eDSU70iywuA.jpg)

Share, feel supported and explore together the transitions we experience and how we show up through them.

  
Dafna leads online process groups for people across the U.S. and internationally. These groups are not therapy groups; they are experiential spaces focused on how we show up in relationships, how we impact and are impacted by others, and how we can engage more effectively and authentically. They are powerful, growth‑oriented communities—and often a more accessible option.
