### _Frequently Asked Questions_

<!-- accordion -->
**Q: Where do I start?**

1.  Check the services section and see what type of service/s best fits your needs
    
2.  Scroll to the bottom of that service page you selected and get to know the providers you want to work with by clicking on ‘About Me’ button.
    
3.  After choosing your provider, click on their “contact” button to schedule a free consultation session that takes between 15-20 minutes either in person or video.
    
4.  When you meet during consultation, this is the time for a (Q&A), or share a brief summary of your needs, or ask them to share their treatment or what to expect.

**Q: Do you accept insurance?**

Our clinicians are primarily private pay. Some may be credentialed with select insurance plans. We encourage you to check with your specific therapist about their accepted insurers. We can provide a superbill (receipt) for you to submit to your insurance for potential out-of-network reimbursement.

**Q: Do you offer telehealth (online therapy)?**

Yes! All of our clinicians offer telehealth video sessions, and many also offer in-person sessions at our Bellevue office (14655 NE Bel-Red Road, Building F, Suite 203). We serve clients throughout Washington State via telehealth.

**Q: Do you see children, teens, and adults?**

Yes. Our diverse team serves clients across all stages of life — from early childhood through adulthood. We have specialists in child and teen therapy (including selective mutism and anxiety), adult therapy, couples therapy, family therapy, and more.

**Q: What languages do your therapists speak?**

Our team speaks multiple languages including English, Hebrew, Tagalog, Spanish, Russian, and Ukrainian. We welcome clients from diverse cultural backgrounds and are committed to culturally sensitive care.

**Q: How do I get matched with the right therapist?**

Email us at [therapy@rtcbellevue.com](mailto:therapy@rtcbellevue.com) with a brief description of what you are looking for. Our team will review and match you with the provider whose expertise, availability, and approach best fit your needs. You can also browse individual therapist profiles on our Team page.

**Q: Where are you located, and do you serve my area?**

Our office is located at 14655 NE Bel-Red Road, Building F, Suite 203, Bellevue, WA 98007. We serve clients in-person from Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Sammamish, Mercer Island, and surrounding areas. We also offer telehealth to clients anywhere in Washington State.
