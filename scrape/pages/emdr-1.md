#### **How EMDR Works**

EMDR therapy is based on the Adaptive Information Processing (AIP)model, which suggests that trauma can cause memories to become "stuck" or "frozen" in the brain in a raw, unprocessed state. EMDR uses Bilateral Stimulation (BLS)—typically side-to-side eye movements, hand tapping, or auditory tones—to help the brain's two hemispheres communicate and resume natural healing. 

-   **Minimal Talking:** Unlike traditional talk therapy, you do not need to describe the trauma in detail.
    
-   **Rapid Results:** Many people report significant improvement in as few as 3 to 12 sessions.
    
-   **No Homework:** Most EMDR protocols do not require assignments between sessions.
