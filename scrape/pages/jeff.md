### _Practice Overview_

Dr. Gillman provides psychotherapy and mental health services to teens, adults, and families. His practice is centered on providing developmentally informed, cognitive-behavioral, mindfulness based, and integrative psychotherapy.

He particularly enjoys working with teens, adults, and families who are experiencing health-related issues and traumatic events. Areas of focus include helping clients adjust to and cope with acute or chronic medical diagnoses, preparing for medical procedures, improving adherence to medical regimens, and offering non-pharmacological treatment for pain.

Dr. Gillman serves a diverse range of clients, including teens and adults coping with conditions such as cancer, diabetes, asthma, recurrent headaches, inflammatory bowel disease/recurrent abdominal pain (IBD/RAP), trauma, and PTSD. His expertise also extends to working with first responders and individuals following military deployment.

# About Dr. Gillman

**Professional Background**

Dr. Gillman received his doctorate (Ph.D.) in Clinical Psychology from Virginia Commonwealth

University / The Medical College of Virginia, with extensive training in assessment, intervention, and consultation for adults, adolescents, and children. After completing his clinical internship at the Indiana University School of Medicine Consortium of Clinical Psychology, he joined the faculty and taught at the Riley Children’s Hospital Child Development Center. He then pursued further training, completing a Postdoctoral Fellowship in Pediatric Psychology at The University of Oklahoma Health Sciences Center. Following his fellowship, Dr. Gillman joined the faculty at Nationwide Children’s Hospital, affiliated with The Ohio State University School of Medicine, where he served as Clinical Assistant Professor of Pediatrics and Director of the Consultation-Liaison Service for eight years. Upon relocating to the Northwest, he spent six years as a member of the Pediatric Psychology Service at Mary Bridge Children’s Hospital in Tacoma, where he developed and directed the Mary Bridge Multidisciplinary Eating Disorders Program. In 2003, Dr. Gillman transitioned to private practice.

**Additional Qualifications and Contributions:**

In addition to his clinical and teaching roles, Dr. Gillman has co-authored a professional text focused on supporting children with acute and chronic health issues. He has published several articles in professional journals and has presented at numerous public speaking engagements, covering topics such as parenting children with medical challenges, eating disorders, clinical uses of hypnosis for symptom management, and acute and chronic pain management. Dr. Gillman is also a Certified Trainer in Disaster Mental Health, has served as a member of the King County Public Health Medical Reserve Corps, and provides clinical services to U.S. Military Veterans through the Wounded Warrior Project and Give an Hour.
